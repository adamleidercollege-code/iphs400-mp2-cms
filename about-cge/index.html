About CGE

The Kenyon Center for Global Engagement helps students find a program abroad, get ready to go, and make sense of it once they are back.

This site is a demo build for IPHS 400 Mini-Project #2, not an official Kenyon page. The programs, countries, and student dispatches on it are invented, but the structure matches what a real CGE site would need.

  • Advising

    Talk through which programs fit your major, what credits transfer, and how a semester away fits the rest of your degree. Drop in during open hours or book a longer meeting.

  • Pre-departure and re-entry

    Required sessions before you go, covering visas, health, money, and safety, and a smaller one when you get back, where returning students compare notes with people about to leave.

  • Student ambassadors

    Students who have already been abroad write the dispatches on this site and answer questions from anyone considering the same program.
