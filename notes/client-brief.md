# Client brief: Kenyon Center for Global Engagement

This site should be run by the Kenyon Center for Global Engagement. They are fairly comfortable with computers but it should be relatively easy and intuitive to navigate.

The people reading the site are Kenyon students (or students from other universities) looking for information on different study abroad programs. They want to read blog posts on the experience, as well as specific posts tailored towards questions about housing, meal plans, social life, academics, and other hot topics.

As of right now, Kenyon does not have a site like this and information about study abroad programs has to come from outside sources and sites, not always well maintained and often difficult to navigate for specific information. This is a great system because it allows students to easily get real information about study abroad programs they are interested in, tailored to their specific questions and directly from other students who have attended said programs at Kenyon.

CGE staff will take the administrative roles, adding students with permissions to write blog posts but not edit the site further than that. CGE staff have full administrative privileges, allowing them to add, remove, and change other users, as well as write posts and create pages themselves. Ideally, they would reach out to students going abroad to be ambassadors (possibly a paid position) in which they write a certain amount of blog posts throughout their time abroad.

The site would have a home page to orient students, and then would be organized into continent pages, each which would have the countries in which Kenyon provides programs, and within those individual country pages would be program pages with blog posts. Those program pages would have general blog posts, as well as a couple of sections relating to the aforementioned hot topics. These posts would be sorted by date. I would also like to add a search function, which would work for countries, programs, and posts and have standard filters (date, relevance).

What must not happen: a student ambassador must never be able to publish a post without CGE review, or edit or delete anyone else's posts, and nothing unapproved should ever appear on the public site.

## What I'd add next

- Private messaging so students can ask ambassadors follow-up questions without contact info going public. This needs a live server.
- A visitor account role so readers can comment on posts and send messages.
- An IT/developer role that can edit the site's layout and code, separate from CGE admins.
- Search filters by views and comments, which need a live server to track them.
